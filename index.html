How to write for the Director

Global style: define the visual language once.
Character bible: name, age, role, appearance, wardrobe, personality and voice.
Scenes: one major story beat per numbered scene. Describe visible actions in order.
Dialogue: keep speaker names exact and put the spoken words in quotes.
Continuity: say what must remain unchanged across scenes.
Sound: describe natural ambience and important effects.
Do not write 60 tiny scenes. Let AHM create production shots inside each story scene.
