✍️ How to write a GPU-friendly AHM screenplay

You write the story. AHM Director converts it into production shots. The clearer your screenplay, the less guessing the director and GPU pipeline have to do.

1. GLOBAL STYLE
STYLE: Cinematic live-action fantasy, realistic actors, natural faces, dramatic lighting, realistic ocean, film-quality camera work.
2. CHARACTER BIBLE
CHARACTER: ELIAS
ROLE: 45-year-old fisherman
LOOK: Lean build, short dark hair, short beard, worn beige shirt...
PERSONALITY: Kind, humble, patient.
VOICE: Warm, deep, gentle male voice.
3. ONE CLEAR SCENE
SCENE 1 — THE POOR FISHERMAN
LOCATION: Small fishing village beside the ocean. Early morning.
ACTION:
Elias leaves his wooden house carrying an old net.
Mara watches from the doorway.
DIALOGUE:
MARA: "Elias, please catch something today."
ELIAS: "I will, Mara."
CAMERA: Wide village shot, then medium two-shot.
EMOTION: Worried but loving.
SOUND: Ocean waves, morning wind.
4. CONTINUITY
CONTINUITY:
Elias keeps the same face, age, hair, beard and clothing established in the character bible.
Mara keeps the same face, age, hair and dress.
Do not change props or location unless the story says so.
🔥 Best practice: describe what happens visibly, keep dialogue exact, avoid contradictory instructions, and separate major story beats into numbered scenes. AHM can create many internal shots inside one scene; you do not need to write 60 tiny scenes.
